Section 3

Three rules do all the work

1

A play is a customer problem, not a technology

Six problems account for the entire portfolio. A seller places a customer by recognising the problem in the conversation, then picks a size. Nothing in the library is named after a vendor or a platform.

2

Size is a tier, not a new offer

Assess, Build, Run. Most of the 572 offers are the same play at a different size or with a different logo on it. Tiering removes the need for a named offer per size.

3

Managed services are the Run tier, not a play

222 offers in the master are managed, hosted or as-a-service variants. They belong under the play they operate, with the OEM chosen underneath as an attachment, not as a separate offer.
